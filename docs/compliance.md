# Privacy and custody checks

Checked 13 November 2026. These checks find missing records. They do not certify legal compliance. The operator decides which laws apply and whether the evidence is adequate.

| Check | Source and meaning | What this desk checks |
|---|---|---|
| PURPOSE and RETENTION_REVIEW | [NZ Privacy Act principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/) limits retention to the time needed for a lawful purpose. | Recipient purpose missing, review date absent or passed. A review date is your local policy, not a statutory retention period. |
| ACCESS_EVIDENCE | [NZ principle 5](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/5/) requires reasonable safeguards for personal information. | A reference to the access review is missing. A reference alone does not prove the safeguards work. |
| AU_RETENTION | [Australian APP 11](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-11-app-11-security-of-personal-information) addresses security and, subject to exceptions, destruction or de-identification when information is no longer needed. | Australian recipient records whose retention review is absent or overdue. Recipient country does not establish APP applicability; assess the organisation separately. |
| LOAN_TERMS | Local custody policy, not legislation. | Reservations without a terms reference; dispatch is blocked until terms and destination are recorded. |

The thirty-day stocktake interval and sixty-day idle threshold are local demo policies. Reservation dates include both endpoints. A same-day handover to another borrower requires a deliberate rule change; the default leaves a return-day buffer.

Keep recipient details to business contact information. Do not put home addresses, identity documents or private material into notes. Generated reports and exports are private, local files; review who receives them. Row security blocks public access by default. The database owner connection is privileged and needs authenticated access controls before shared use. The actor argument records attribution and is not a login or signature.

The history is append-only during normal work. A privacy officer must review required retention, then use a separately authorised migration to remove or de-identify information from contacts, historical snapshots, drafts, exports and backups together. No automatic deletion is performed. This desk does not determine customs duty, issue tax invoices or replace the recipient's signed loan agreement.
