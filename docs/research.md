# Research and selection

Checked 13 November 2026. Family: sample circulation. This is a physical PR sample desk, separate from apparel development, commercial hire, media monitoring and influencer discovery.

## Pricing and buyer evidence

[Launchmetrics regional Korea pricing](https://launchmetrics.kr/en/launchmetrics-pricing-solutions) lists Essentials from EUR 1,550 monthly, billed annually, for up to two modules and three seats. Twelve months is EUR 18,600. This is a regional package benchmark, not a Samples-only price, an ANZ quote or a customer invoice. The same page lists Enterprise from EUR 3,250 monthly. [Global pricing](https://www.launchmetrics.com/launchmetrics-pricing-solutions) separately lists Starter from EUR 780 monthly and quote-based higher packages. Those different scopes and regions are not interchangeable.

Scores in order: annual bill 2, data-shaped 5, our buyers 3, search demand 2, one-session buildable 5, import path 3. Total 20/30. No measured keyword volumes or verified EDNA customer invoice. Local customer and CRM snapshots had no verified subscription; no live CRM access was used.

[Flaunter's Australian agency case study](https://www.flaunter.com/case-study/awpr) describes a Sydney agency previously using Launchmetrics for sample tracking. It establishes a relevant small-agency workflow, not a current subscription or its price. [G2 reviewer feedback](https://www.g2.com/products/launchmetrics/reviews) describes sample process terminology that did not fit one team's own wording. Treat that as one user's experience, not a universal product flaw. Search listings show comparison intent, not a measured search-demand figure.

## Scope and import

[Launchmetrics Samples](https://www.launchmetrics.com/software/samples-management) distinguishes individual physical samples from PLM product concepts. Its [tracking guide](https://www.launchmetrics.com/resources/blog/sample-tracking-software) describes loans, transfers, reservations, archiving, gifting and write-offs, along with barcode, season and location fields. This build implements reservations, loans, partial returns, stocktakes, archive/write-off records and reports. It is not a complete copy of the suite.

[Sample inventory permissions](https://www.launchmetrics.com/resources/product-news/samples-new-permissions) describes inventory consistency and bulk import. Public standard export headings were not found; the importer uses a documented local interchange mapping and preserves every source column. Export capabilities are also described by reviewers, but no customer export was available for a migration acceptance test.

Alternatives screened: Visier people analytics 17/30, lower small-business fit and no verified public small-business price. EnergyCAP 20/30, rejected for overlap with the existing energy-management family. Easygenerator rejected for lower published team bill and an authoring scope that needs a different product. No other target has been queued by this run.
